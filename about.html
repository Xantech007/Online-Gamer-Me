About Online Gamer®

Where Classic Arcade Gaming Meets Real Time Rewards

Founded with a passion for retro web gaming, Online Gamer® turns casual gaming sessions into rewarding experiences. We host a curated selection of lightweight, high-performance web arcade games that run seamlessly across mobile and desktop browsers.

Our Platform Model

We believe players should be rewarded for their time and skill. By tracking active gameplay and score achievements in real time, Online Gamer® allows registered users to accumulate balance directly into their account wallet while enjoying timeless arcade classics.

🎮 Retro Library

Access classics ranging from strategy and puzzles like 2048 and Eight Queens to fast-paced action like Hextris and Clumsy Bird.

⚡ Real-Time Tracking

Integrated Cloud Firestore infrastructure monitors active playtime and stats instantly as you play.

📱 Cross-Platform

Optimized for low bandwidth and full responsiveness across desktop keyboards and mobile touchscreens.

💳 Instant Withdrawals

Easily manage your earnings with low minimum thresholds and simple payout requests right from your profile.

Security & Transparency

Our infrastructure utilizes industry-standard Firebase authentication and strict database security rules to safeguard player accounts, balances, and session history. Every payout request is reviewed to ensure fair play across our leaderboard community.
