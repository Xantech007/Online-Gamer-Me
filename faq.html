Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about your account, plans, gameplay, and payout requests.

We offer different tier memberships, such as the Basic Plan and Silver Plan, which unlock higher daily earning caps, exclusive mini-games, and faster payout speeds. You can view or upgrade your active plan anytime inside your Profile dashboard.

Once you reach the minimum balance threshold for your tier, navigate to the Withdraw page via the main navigation bar. Enter your payout credentials and submit the form. Processing times depend on your membership plan level.

Rewards are accrued based on active playtime, achievements unlocked, and match victories. Tiered plan multipliers boost your base earning rate during special daily match events.

To ensure fair gameplay and payout security, each player is strictly limited to one primary account. Creating duplicate accounts may result in an automated hold on payout processing.

Ensure you have a stable network connection and clear your browser cache. If score recording fails due to a network glitch, contact support with a screenshot or details via the Contact page or floating support widget.

Standard processing fees vary according to your designated payment gateway. Premium plan members receive discounted or waived transaction processing fees.
